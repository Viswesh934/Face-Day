# Face Day — Demonstration Specification & Video Script

## Target Track: Creativity Track
**Event:** Presage SmartSpectra SDK Challenge on DEV Community

---

## 1. Core Narrative & High-Concept Pitch
> *"In a comic universe divided by thunder and steel, your live vitals dictate your faction. Are you worthy of the Asgardian Hammer, or destined for the Latverian Mask?"*

Face Day transforms contactless physiological sensing into a tangible, collectible trading card game. Rather than presenting numbers in a clinical dashboard, it translates micro-fluctuations in blood volume, breathing, and facial tension into RPG stats and minted collectible cards.
